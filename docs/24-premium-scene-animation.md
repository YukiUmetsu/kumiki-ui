## Creative directions: 22 candidates with explicit timing status

These are **proposed directions**, not claims about what the current implementation renders. The review table below records the inspected implementation and its unresolved gates. Each direction is a reusable shot grammar: swap the subject, environment, media and editorial language without baking a particular industry or story into the component. Choose a camera move because it reveals information; a locked shot, a motivated cut and silence can be more cinematic than constant movement.

A premium production begins with an authored shot list, visual references, real scale, lighting continuity, lens and depth-of-field choices, color grade, precise sound and a deliberate hold on the payoff. Separate narration, music and effects on a common frame timeline; leave room for voice and license every track. Text is measured in its actual font and output aspect, with protected areas for captions, source credits and moving subjects. Explainers retain one observable input → transformation → output per beat. A scene must also work in a reduced-motion edit. Native 3D, footage, compositing, simulation and sound are production dependencies where described—not capabilities implied by the current 2D fallback. Apply the [visual production contract](visual-production-contract.md), [3D fidelity gate](14-3d-visual-fidelity.md) and [asset sourcing rules](ASSET_SOURCING.md).

## Earth geography and visual behavior (new code-review result)

Earlier the globe image and the projected markers moved in unrelated coordinate systems; a viewer could see a marker drifting over the wrong continent. This has been addressed by **a single camera projection** used by graticules, public-domain Natural Earth 110m coastline polylines, city markers and great-circle routes. All geometry is evaluated at the absolute composition frame, with a separate fixed-camera comparison mode and a reduced-motion fallback. Test cases now cover horizon clipping, random-access determinism, dateline crossing, antipodal routes, and coastline rotation.

**Limitations:** The Earth remains a stylized orthographic 2.5D composition rather than a real shader-driven 3D sphere; coastline outlines are simplified with 0.25° tolerance. The output is unsuitable for political borders or surveying. `globe.src` is no longer incorrectly projected as a rotating land texture in geographic mode. Verify these claims visually on exported normal-speed clips before premium QA signoff.
