# East Asian transformation makeup (MAKEUP1) — research, 2026-11-17

The owner asked for trendy East Asian looks that almost transform a face: eyes that look bigger, longer lashes and
drawn shadows. This note collects the evidence and the visual targets for five looks. The design is in
[the design document](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-east-asian-makeup-design.md).

Grades:
- **reported:** quoted from a study.
- **convention:** makeup-artist, beauty-media or product practice, consistent across sources, not tested.
- **estimated:** an amount chosen within what the sources support.
- **observed:** seen in the reference photographs listed below (local study only).

## The looks

### Chinese social-video glam (`c-glam`; popularly "Douyin makeup")

Sources: makeup.com, Vogue Singapore, Refinery29, Daily Vanity and StyleBible (convention). Elements:
- aegyo-sal highlight, with a brown shadow on its lower edge and shimmer or glitter on it.
- an inner-corner liner, drawn down toward the inner corner.
- drawn lower lashes, or false lashes set on the aegyo-sal line.
- a puppy or level wing.
- blush under the eyes and across the nose bridge, which visually shortens the mid-face.
- a narrow nose contour, a porcelain base and gradient lips.

Visual targets (observed):
- warm peach to orange-brown shadow wrapped round the eye, on the upper lid and the outer lower lid.
- a thin black liner a little past the outer corner, near level.
- separated spiky upper lashes and defined lower lashes.
- large dark-brown irises.
- peach-pink blush high under the eyes.
- glossy coral gradient lips.

### Korean idol (`k-idol`)

Sources: L'Oréal Paris (ulzzang), Time Out, Pinkvilla (convention). Elements:
- straight brows and a downward puppy liner.
- an aegyo-sal highlight along the lower lash line.
- glowing skin.
- a gradient lip: darker at the inner centre, fading out.

Visual targets (observed):
- hazel, olive or brown lenses with a dark ring.
- a short, thin wing, level to a little down.
- curled natural lashes with defined lower lashes.
- an aegyo-sal band.
- soft, straight brows.
- pink blush high on the cheeks.
- a glossy nose bridge (glass skin) and glossy coral-pink gradient lips.

### Jirai-kei (`jirai`)

Sources: FUN! JAPAN, Shoptery (convention). Elements:
- a liner that droops down from the outer corner.
- pink or red shadow along and below the lower lash line.
- heavy lower lashes.
- pink or berry blush high, close under the eyes.
- a pale base.

Visual targets (observed, from a CC BY 3.0 jirai makeup video):
- a saturated round pink-red blush directly under each eye.
- a reddish lower lid and a red-brown drooping liner.
- large dark lenses.
- a very pale matte base.
- small deep-red gradient lips.

### Neo-gyaru (`neo-gyaru`)

Sources: FUN! JAPAN, Galture (convention). Elements:
- false lashes and tinted lenses for doll-like eyes.
- black liner round the whole eye, with a light inner rim.
- a sweet drooping outer corner (tareme).
- tear-bag shading in the newer, lighter version.

Visual targets (observed: 2007-2015 Shibuya and Ikebukuro street photographs, and an illustration):
- heavy black liner all round the eye.
- dense long lashes, upper and lower.
- large light or dark lenses.
- nude pink lips.
- a tanned or very fair base.

### Ryousangata (`ryousangata`)

Sources: Japanese beauty media on tear-bag and drooping-eye makeup: biteki, onecosme, MAQUIA (convention). Elements:
- a tear bag with a soft line under it.
- a brown liner about 5 mm longer than the eye and lowered at the outer corner.
- a red-brown second liner on the inner half.
- pink tones.

Visual targets (observed: Japanese idol advertising photographs, the makeup this style copies):
- soft pink-brown lids and a thin brown drooping liner.
- an aegyo-sal band with a soft pinkish-brown shadow.
- a lightly defined lower lash line.
- brown lenses with a ring.
- glossy coral-pink gradient lips and pink-fair skin.

## Lens size in photographs: a negative result

MediaPipe's iris landmarks gave a visible iris of 0.43-0.49 eye widths in the made-up references. The same detector
gave 0.46 for the bare East Asian faces of the Face Research Lab London Set (n = 9) and 0.44 overall (n = 102). So the
detector does not see circle lenses. It fits an iris of a normal size. The lens sizes therefore come from the
products and from Takehara & Tanijiri 2015, not from these photographs.

## What the renders changed

| First result | Fixed by |
| --- | --- |
| Strokes away from the lid (wing tips, the aegyo-sal shadow, the tear mole) did not reach the skin: their points floated in front of the face or behind it. | `surface: true` points: a point takes the depth of the skin under it (TypeScript and Python, with a parity test). |
| Three aegyo-sal points drew a V-shaped triangle. | An 8-point crescent along the lower lid, each point set down by its own radius. |
| The aegyo-sal read as a pale ring round the eye and covered the lower liner. | A softer edge, shimmer at lower coverage, and the aegyo-sal painted before the liners. |
| Long hair and a key light from 30 degrees put one eye in shadow, and the makeup on it was not visible. | Review and story portraits use a bun and a key light from 15 degrees (beauty light). |
| The first looks were too weak to see at face size: the eyes did not change. | Stronger liners and wings, lenses, styled lashes and blush; each look checked side by side with its references. |
| A straight brow zone drew a thin wedge. | Straight upper and lower edges, with the tail raised. |
| The gyaru black lower liner did not show: the lower-lash zone is a smudge under the outer half only, and the lower lashes hid it. | A new `liner-lower` zone: a fine line along the whole lower lash line, from the outer corner. The gyaru eye now has liner all round it, joined to the wing. |
| The gyaru cheekbone highlight showed as pale patches at the sides of the face. | Coverage 0.35 with a dewy finish instead of 0.6 shimmer. |
| The Korean idol and ryousangata blush sat at the outer cheeks; the references show it under the eyes. | More `blush-igari` (under the eyes) and less of the apple or lifted placements. |
| The Chinese glam eye had no line under its outer half. | A brown `liner-lower` on the outer half (scale 0.45). |

## Limits

- The style definitions are conventions from beauty media and practitioners, not tested.
- The jirai-kei and gyaru references are few. One gyaru reference is an illustration.
- The lens effect on perceived size has one study (Takehara & Tanijiri 2015, digitally enlarged irises, not lenses).
- No human visual review yet.
