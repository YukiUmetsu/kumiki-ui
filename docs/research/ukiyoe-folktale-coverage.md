# UKIYO1 folktale coverage (100 stories)

Owner request (2026-11-15): make animated ukiyo-e YouTube videos of the 100 stories in the owner's folktale database ("100 Old Japanese Folktales — Story & Animation Database"). This page maps each story to the art the pack already has and to the roadmap slices (F1–F16 in [the roadmap](../06-roadmap.md#ukiyo-e-handscroll-ukiyo1--folktale-library-queue-2026-11-15), plus F17, the episode template every story uses) that add what is missing. Slice numbers are labels; the roadmap orders them by how many stories each unlocks.

**Already covered by the pack:** peach, oni and kanabō fights, dog, monkey, pheasant, fox and nine-tailed fox, tanuki, cat, rabbit, horse, crane, heron, crow, sparrow, dragon, snow woman, yūrei, rokurokubi, kappa, tengu, nue, nurikabe, tsuchigumo, umbrella and lantern spirits, soul-flames, samurai, katana and duels, the oiran and maiko, townsfolk and crowds, castles and castle towns, farmhouses, jizō, komainu, torii, pagodas, bridges, boats, the great wave, the sea, mountains, rain, snow, fireworks and recorded sound beds. Stories 12, 46, 83, 84 and 90 can be made with the pack as it is.
