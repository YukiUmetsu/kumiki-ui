## Actual Chromium v10 rejection and restrained wax-root v11 (2026-11-15)

**Reviewed source-native proof:** [a2d4d878 run 43779294634](https://github.com/YukiUmetsu/kumiki-ui/actions/runs/43779294634), [artifact 14187244864](https://github.com/YukiUmetsu/kumiki-ui/actions/runs/43779294634/artifacts/14187244864), paired 608×1082 source-value/source-sculpt Chromium PNGs at the **same near (2.95×)** and **far (3×)** camera at f299. Earlier source photographs and v10 output were examined as actual pixels, not guessed from code. **Artist verdict: REJECT.** The v10 new dark lash-root geometry produces an unnaturally **thick eyeliner/eyebrow-like slab** with a visibly separated **bright pink cut-paper skin band** above a geometric iris. In the far-eye macro the thick dark ribbon worsens the reference mismatch. Keeping the 47 numerical root endpoints attached to the tarsal rail did **not** make the image look like the dense but fine photographed eyelashes.

These are **A/B magnitude diagnostics, not proof of improvement over the photograph**: near local eye ROI x140..480/y470..642 mean absolute RGB/channel difference about **12.91/255**, >8/channel pixel-change fraction **41.24%**; far local eye ROI difference about **9.77/255**, >8 fraction **39.76%**. The many changed pixels largely represent *new unconvincing wax*, not more accurate features.

**Causal next candidate (v11, still unapproved):** keep every existing scene/iris/47 fiber/path/timeline/paper-seed and optical tooth intact; remodel the two tarsal-shelf lower skin edges so they blend toward (not stand distinctly above) the dark upper lash margin; narrow the central dark lash-root wax to an irregular pigmented rim, deliberately reduce excess `opaqueBurnish` from ~0.85 to ~0.3 while preserving opaque material and pressure texture, and concentrate the additional feathered burnish into shorter source-curved patches with softer pigment contact. This corrects *overpainting*, not by deleting anatomical shapes or flattening detailed regions. Near/far remain different. No use of opaque image stickers, photo masking, ambient blur or source-traced bitmaps.
