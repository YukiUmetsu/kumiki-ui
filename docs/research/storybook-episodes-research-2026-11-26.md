# Research for 8-minute storybook episodes (2026-11-26)

Evidence for [the design](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-storybook-eight-minute-episodes-design.md). Grades: **A** = peer-reviewed study, meta-analysis or official operator or government page. **B** = broadcaster, curriculum, recognised practitioner, reputable guide or newspaper. **C** = blog, forum or one creator. "Inference" marks a rule that we take from the evidence and that no source states. Checked on 2026-11-26.

## 1. Format and attention

| Finding | Grade | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Median engagement is at most 6 minutes, whatever the length of the video. Viewers often stop before half of a video that is longer than 9 minutes. Use chunks shorter than 6 minutes, with clear marks at each change. | A | Guo, Kim & Rubin 2014, https://www.cs.rochester.edu/hci/pubs/pdfs/edX-MOOC-video-production-and-engagement_LAS-2014.pdf |
| Segmenting helps (56 studies), also when the video sets the breaks. | A | Rey et al. 2019, https://www.doi.org/10.1007/S10648-018-9456-4 (abstract) |
| Signalling the key content: d = .38. | A | Alpizar, Adesope & Wong 2020 (abstract), https://rex.libraries.wsu.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/A-meta-analysis-of-signaling-principle-in/99900601052901842 |
| A test after each 5-minute part of a lecture halved mind wandering (about 40 % to 20 %). A 2016 follow-up found no difference against restudy. Practice tests beat restudy. | A | Szpunar, Khan & Schacter 2013; Jing, Szpunar & Schacter 2016, https://sites.harvard.edu/schacter-memory/files/2022/08/jing2016-1-1.pdf; Adesope et al. 2017 |
| A delay before the first recall improves recall two days later. Expanding against equal gaps has little effect. | A | Karpicke & Roediger 2007 |
| Spacing across sessions beats massing (g = 1.51 against 0.97; at the delayed test 1.71 against 0.58). Watching alone teaches about 7 % of the target words, and basic learners gain less. | A | Webb, Uchihara & Yanagisawa 2023, https://cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/S0261444822000507 |
| How often a word comes predicts learning (r = .34). | A | Uchihara, Webb & Yanagisawa 2019, https://doi.org/10.1111/lang.12343 |
| No study gives the best pause for an answer in a video. Classroom wait time: 3 s or more. | C | Rowe 1986 summary, IRIS Vanderbilt |
| Shadowing helps lower-level learners more. Four to five repetitions reach a plateau in accuracy. Japanese intonation can be learned by shadowing (38 learners). | A | Hashizaki et al. 2025, https://jalt-publications.org/sites/default/files/pdf-article/jj47.1-art1.pdf; Sonu 2018 (abstract) |
| NHK Easy Japanese (48 lessons, A1–A2): a skit with English, Japanese or no subtitles, vocabulary, a key phrase with a can-do goal, a dialogue to repeat, a three-way choice, then the phrase with new words. Text shows Japanese, romaji and English. | B | https://www3.nhk.or.jp/nhkworld/lesson/en/about/howtouse/ |
| Comprehensible-input channels (Comprehensible Japanese, Teppei, Dreaming Spanish) are about 5 minutes, all in the target language, with drawings and no drills. | B/C | https://www.dreaming.com/method; Tofugu database |

## 2. When English shows

| Finding | Grade | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Captions in the language that is learned help listening (g = 0.99) and vocabulary (g = 0.87), at each level. | A | Montero Perez et al. 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.system.2013.07.013 |
| Captions beat L1 subtitles for the form of a word. L1 subtitles do not beat captions for its meaning. | A | Peters, Heynen & Puimège 2016 (abstract) |
| Bilingual subtitles beat captions for recognising meaning and L1 subtitles for recalling it, but lose on form. Only attention to the target words predicted gains. A translation suits lower levels more. | A | Wang & Pellicer-Sánchez 2022, https://doi.org/10.1111/lang.12495 |

Inference: English under the Japanese in the story, the Japanese first and the English after a pause in the reviews, no English in a check until the answer, and a last pass in Japanese only.

## 3. Camera work instead of body acting

| Finding | Grade | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tezuka's studio kept costs down by using drawings again and by fewer drawings a second. | B | BFI, https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/features/looping-around-limitations-experimental-shorts-tezuka-osamu |
| Ken Burns treats a still as a long shot and moves inside it to a medium shot or a close-up; he warns against moving for the sake of moving. | B | Poynter, https://www.poynter.org/news/meaning-motion-ken-burns-and-his-effect |
| Ozu's pillow shots of objects and places hold for some seconds between scenes. Miyazaki: action with no breathing space is "just busyness". | B | BFI; Roger Ebert interview |
| Pan no faster than one image width in 7 s at 24 fps. | B | RED, https://www.reddigitalcinema.com/red-101/camera-panning-speed |
| A subtitle ends two frames before a cut, or at least 0.5 s after it. | B | Netflix timed-text guide, https://backlothelp.netflix.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051554394 |

No study gives the best shot length for adult learners. Working rules (inference): one place for each chapter; an establishing or still-life shot holds 4–6 s; an insert holds 2–4 s; a dialogue shot holds for its whole line; a pan takes 7 s or more for one frame width; one small change (steam, a slow push, a cut) every 5–10 s.

## 4. Facts of the places

Grades as above. Each episode uses only these facts or a new source.

**Kyoto Station and the Tokaido Shinkansen**
- The shinkansen gates are on the south (Hachijō) side, 新幹線八条口, and 新幹線中央口 on the 2nd-floor passage. Tracks 11–12 go to Tokyo, 13–14 to Shin-Osaka (C, ekitan.com).
- A paper ticket is two tickets, 乗車券 and 特急券. Both go into the gate together. The exit gate keeps them (A, https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/tickets/use).
- Since 15 Mar 2025 only cars 1–2 of a Nozomi are non-reserved (B, travel.watch.impress.co.jp).
- A bag of 161–250 cm (length + width + height) needs a seat with an oversized-baggage area. Without one: 1,000 yen (A, https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/info/oversized-baggage).
- Tokyo–Kyoto on a Nozomi: 14,170 yen reserved, 13,320 yen non-reserved, normal season (A, JR West table of 16 Oct 2026). The reserved charge rises 700 yen on 1 Jul 2027.
- Children aged 6 to 11 pay half the fare and the limited-express charge (rounded down to 5 yen). Ages 1 to 5 ride free unless they take a reserved seat, which needs a child fare (A, https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/tickets/type/adults_children.html).
- Top speed 285 km/h. Average delay 1.6 minutes a train in FY2023. 372 trains a day (A, https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/company/about_shinkansen).
- Seat E sees Mount Fuji, about 1 h 26 min after Kyoto toward Tokyo (C).
- **The trolley sales ended on 31 Oct 2023.** Use the vending machines on the platform (B, watch.impress.co.jp).
- **The bowing cleaning crew is at Tokyo (JR East), not at Kyoto.** Do not paint it at Kyoto (A, C).

**Konbini**
- Bags cost money since 1 Jul 2020 (A, METI). Large chains: 3 yen small, 5–7 yen large (C, June 2024).
- Take-out food 8 %, eat-in 10 % tax; the shop asks at the till (A, NTA).
- Clerk lines: ポイントカードはお{持|も}ちですか, お{温|あたた}めしますか, お{箸|はし}はお{付|つ}けしますか, {袋|ふくろ}にお{入|い}れしますか (B, livejapan.com, Feb 2026).
- Onigiri cost about 196–232 yen (C, Oct 2026). Not verified: the numbered wrapper, IC-card payment.

**Souvenir shop**
- Staff often ask ご{自宅用|じたくよう}ですか. The answer: プレゼント{用|よう}です (B).
- **Tax-free shopping is a refund since 1 Nov 2026**: 5,000 yen or more before tax, in one shop on one day; pay the full price; scan the passport at a customs terminal at the airport; the shop refunds. Goods used in Japan get no refund (A, https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/tax-free/page01_000001_00028.html).
- Kyō-sensu: only members of the makers' cooperative use the name (A, Kyoto Prefecture). Nama-yatsuhashi keeps about 12 days, baked about 120 (C). A tenugui 1,155 yen, a silk fan 1,760 yen (C).

**Summer festival** (our film is a neighbourhood 夏祭り, not Gion Matsuri: Gion has no fireworks)
- Takoyaki: the pan at about 220 °C; batter, octopus, tenkasu; each ball turned 90° at a time; sauce, aonori, katsuobushi; a boat-shaped tray and a pick (A, MAFF).
- Stall prices: takoyaki and yakisoba 500–700 yen (C, 2023 survey).
- Yukata: the left panel over the right (B).

**Taxi in Kyoto**
- Use a stand; do not hail at a crossing. Foreign Friendly Taxi stands at Kyoto Station take cards (A, kyoto.travel).
- The driver opens and closes the left rear door (B).
- Since 6 Aug 2025: at most 500 yen for the first 0.9 km, then 100 yen for each 255 m (A, MLIT Kinki).

**Ryokan**
- Check-in usually from 15:00. Shoes off in the genkan; slippers off on tatami; toilet slippers (B, C).
- Dinner at 18:00 or 19:00, confirmed at check-in (B).
- Bath: wash first, the small towel stays out of the water, long hair tied up (A, japan.travel).
- Kyoto lodging tax since 1 Mar 2026: 200 to 10,000 yen a person a night by room price (A, kyoto.travel).
