# Paper Craft scenery — reference study and construction audit

Date: 2026-11-14. ArtCraft method: study original work → identify transferable physical construction → design an original composition → author SVG stock → render/test. **No artwork was traced, downloaded into the repository or redistributed.**

## References inspected

1. [Hari & Deepti — Through the Groves and illuminated paper dioramas (Colossal)](https://www.thisiscolossal.com/2024/12/hari-deepti-paper-sculptures/). Nested dark-to-light paper apertures create deep spatial recession. Their carefully cut branches and tiny leaves frame open illuminated space; foreground silhouettes are higher-contrast than distant scenery. **Translation:** actual die-cut oval front mat, attached branch/leaf stock, five opaque landscape planes, color-graded depth using different *sheets*, and readable focal sun.
2. [Hari & Deepti — I Wandered Lonely as a Cloud](https://www.artsy.net/artwork/hari-and-deepti-i-wandered-lonely-as-a-cloud-1). The glowing horizon, stepped hill bands and tall framing forest use light-to-dark layers, controlled opening width and meaningful negative space. **Translation:** stage aperture, layered terrain and curved water corridor; no reproduced figure or original composition.
3. [Helen Musselwhite — working with paper (Creative Boom)](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/helen-musselwhite/) and [woodland countryside examples](https://www.designswan.com/archives/from-graphic-design-to-paper-magic-the-art-of-helen-musselwhite.html). Separate small botanical shapes, sculpted tree trunks and folds create tactile abundance around legible landscape structure. **Translation:** notched grouped conifers, multi-plane trunk/bough joins, hooked fern leaflets and individual flower rosettes rather than scattering colored circular particles.
4. [Eiko Ojala — Vertical Landscape](https://www.saatchiart.com/art/Printmaking-Vertical-Landscape/508401/2259020/view). Restrained hills, near/far form separation and a narrow descending water corridor give distance and scale without a busy backdrop. **Translation:** separate contour-defined snow caps, paper moraine marks and zigzag ridge masses with a wide quiet lake. No shape tracing or artist-specific motifs.
5. [Louise Jenkins — layered paper cuttings (All Things Paper)](https://www.allthingspaper.net/2014/02/layered-paper-cuttings-louise-jenkins.html). Individual rural scenes use several physically stacked paper strata and fine punched details. **Translation:** bounded die-cut microdetail and layer-aware paper shadows, never a transparent photograph.

## Original subject designs

**Woodland window** is an invented moss-green clearing, illuminated sun, five irregular land strata, a widening stream, 2 rooted foreground trunks with independently authored S-branch joins and canopy packets, a midground pine thicket, cut ferns, ripples and small wired flowers. Primary topology rule: every bough starts inside its trunk contour; fern leaflets intersect the continuous central rachis. The scenery has a deep foreground and unobstructed horizon, not a copied shadow-box layout.

**Alpine lake** uses three differentiated mountain masses, real separated snowcap sheets, seeded moraine fragments, joined conifer cuts, steep rock shelves and a broad glacial lake with individual reflection bands. The far shore sits between mountain planes and front ledges, allowing multiple depths of cardstock. Small birds introduce scale. This is an original scene rather than a copy of Eiko Ojala's vertical print.

Both compose within an oval die-cut stage opening that adapts geometrically (no stretched animals/objects) to landscape and portrait. Stock remains six-digit opaque hex, shadows are per-sheet, and all non-kinematic flutter derives from absolute film frames. The same Storybook scenery page exposes preset, three palettes, detail, breeze, paper medium, aspect, tooth, edge, shadow, seed and frame. Detail changes actual cut density, not a noise/color multiplier.

## ArtCraft evaluation contract

- **Topology:** all outer contours must close; aperture cutouts are genuine even-odd holes; no detached generic "tree roots" or synthetic image overlays.
- **Anatomical/structural flow:** trunks link branches, crowns start on branch termini, ferns attach to stalks, alpine pine needles are part of a continuous canopy shape.
- **Stroke/material:** use opaque cardstock; five-plus major depth strata should be readable, and shadows follow the stock depth.
- **Optical rhythm:** multiple differently sized tree types and staggered silhouette positions, no identical trees pasted in rows.
- **Microtexture:** actual cut details (leaflets, snow shards, waterline contours), not global fractal/noise overlays.
- **Motion:** no temporal randomness, shared-hinge flutter only, seek-repeatable and reduced-motion still.
- **Evidence:** 16:9 and 9:16 fully assembled browser captures, variant/seed tests, native PNG frames and encoded film. Those outputs still require manual aesthetic review; automated passing does **not** prove artistic similarity.

## Pass 21 — structural cutwork study (2026-11-14)

### Reference-scale observations and negative controls

- **Helen Musselwhite**, [*A Walk Through the Woods* material and botanical construction](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/helen-musselwhite/) and [layered forest examples](https://makezine.com/article/maker-news/3d-paper-dioramas/): large upper paper foliage cards, carefully lit setbacks, fine joined fronds, recognizable botanical species and actual projected shadows. Scale hierarchy: big canopy masses, rooted stems, overlapping middle-distance fronds, cut botanical microdetail. **Negative control:** two skinny tree trunks with floating postage-stamp leaves and rows of equally sized ferns.
- **Hari & Deepti**, [illuminated multi-depth forest shadowboxes](https://www.designswan.com/archives/enchanting-paper-cut-art-by-hari-deepti-a-world-of-light-and-mystery.html): dark outer aperture, branching arch and lace holes; the distant light must remain readable. **Negative control:** ring of leaves pasted on a flat backing, detached branches, light represented by an opaque white rectangle or translucent whole-tree sprites.
- **Folded mountain-paper examples**, [cut alpine valley compositions](https://www.designswan.com/archives/from-graphic-design-to-paper-magic-the-art-of-helen-musselwhite.html) and countryside papercraft: snow shelf follows summit form, folded-face triangles lead down peaks, treeline is separated from lake and shore. **Negative control:** three identical triangles, a straight shallow water stripe, full-height bank hiding the lake or texture-only depth.

### Before/after structural diagnosis

The pass-20 finished browser captures show **woodland's bare high-altitude branches with too-small foliage**, a clear negative-space mismatch with detailed forest art, and **alpine's lake mostly covered by a full-bottom "far shore" polygon**. A color change or larger paper grain cannot fix these topology errors.

Pass 21 therefore authors:
1. Two **solid upper crown cards**, shaped as asymmetric scalloped silhouettes and pierced by 4–9 actual elongated leaf cutouts. No alpha shortcuts. Connected leaf-rib subshapes branch from continuous anchor paths.
2. Two joined sets of irregular ground vegetation, 8 grouped thickets, finer correctly oriented fern leaflets and a real hand-cut current with individual river-light pieces; original large shapes remain behind this detail.
3. A **narrow shore** that leaves open glacial water instead of filling the entire bottom half of the frame, with a new shaped water-current sheet, paper islet and explicitly rooted reeds.
4. Folded glacial strata oriented to the mountain geometry and joined foreground flowers/calyces; sheet count remains bounded by the PAPER1 128-layer physical-cardstock contract.
5. A \`relief:0..1\` control that directly changes stock depth/shadow offsets (and lightbox parallax), *not* image transparency or global filters. Depth compression preserves the same authored contours.

## Pass 22 — botanical tree taxonomy and silhouette grammar

**Problem in actual Pass 21 renders:** the alpine middle-distance treeline reads as a repeating row of thin, outlined copy-paste triangles. Woodland background pines repeated the same profile; ferns shared identical left/right leaf shapes. Merely increasing small details does not repair silhouette credibility.

**Reference study** (principles only; no traced or bundled art):

- [Helen Musselwhite, botanical paper scenes and A Walk Through the Woods](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/helen-musselwhite/): individual woody crowns and recognizable flora create depth through multiple cut/curled sheets, not repeated icons.
- [Hari & Deepti, luminous forest papercut](https://www.designswan.com/archives/enchanting-paper-cut-art-by-hari-deepti-a-world-of-light-and-mystery.html): foreground boughs, dark silhouette arches, canopy apertures and distant illuminated clearings.
- [Pippa Dyrlaga, botanical single-sheet cutting](https://mymodernmet.com/paper-cutting-art-pippa-dyrlaga-interview/): dense leaf and vein detailing keeps the structural integrity of a single sheet.
- [Maude White, detailed papercut flora and fauna](https://www.thisiscolossal.com/2023/01/maude-white-nature-paper-cuts/): individual cuts remain coherent with broader parent form; ornament is not random image noise.

### Three distinct cut evergreen grammars

| Form | Crown shape | Branch rhythm | Visual difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spruce | Dense 9–12 rows, sharply drooping whorls, compact taper | Tight short return notches | Full conical foliage |
| Fir | 6–9 broad shelves and slower outer-width taper | Wide intervals and nearly horizontal tips | Distinct wide tiers |
| Larch | 7–10 open boughs with narrow center spar | Deep return notches | Light and airy foliage |

Every evergreen is one connected closed cardstock silhouette. Left and right branch asymmetry is individually seeded, fixed across time. Height, width, natural treeline gaps and root landings vary independently. These are paper-illustration conventions, not botanical scans.

A 0–1 treeVariety option controls the use of the three species and composition jitter: 0 is a homogeneous spruce stand, 1 a mixed stand. The seed controls reproducible variation, never per-frame flicker. Deciduous branch tips and fern rachises now receive location-seeded growth variation.

**Negative controls:** identical topology across a mixed treeline, disconnected branch roots, floating trunks, masking the central lake with foreground branches, and frame-to-frame path changes. The unit tests inspect per-species path vertex counts, one contour per tree, seed variation, opacity, portrait/landscape parity and the 128-stock budget. Review actual browser images separately; passing tests cannot establish aesthetic parity.


## Pass 23 — sculpted botany, root topology and floral seedheads

Reference-led observations, not copied source compositions:

- [Helen Musselwhite's botanical wreath](https://www.designswan.com/archives/from-graphic-design-to-paper-magic-the-art-of-helen-musselwhite.html) features overlapping serrated fern edges, wide differences in leaf shape and substantial floral heads. The leaf *contour* matters more than random dot noise.
- [Musselwhite's process](https://helenmusselwhite.com/about/) begins with sketches, proportion and color-coded layers, then hand cutting, scoring, curling and assembling distinct paper sheets. The silhouette hierarchy (large card → branch → leaf → bloom → contrasting seed center) matters more than polygon count.
- [Su Blackwell, Into the Dark Woods](https://longandryle.com/exhibitions/94-into-the-dark-woods-su-blackwell/works/) and [Paper Birches](https://www.artsy.net/artwork/su-blackwell-paper-birches) show connected irregular tree forks and meaningful light-filled negative space, unlike repeated branch stickers.

Implementation: notchedBotanicalLeaf creates closed, axis-directed serrated cut-stock contours; scenicHerb draws a continuous curved stem, connected lateral forks and several asymmetrically notched leaves, with bright midrib scores on a separate cardstock plane; scenicBloom makes seeded petal fans and separate opaque contrasting pistils. At full detail woodland gains 8 rooted herb sheets and Alpine Lake 6; one common lighter stock sheet carries all vein detail per scene. Existing flowers grow larger, with unequal petal contours and visible centers. The original cut paper shapes and material colors are not copies of any referenced work.

Reject the negative controls: detached foliage, perfectly repeated identical leaf icons, daisy-like dots with no pistils, flowers outside the land, all foliage obscuring water, and shadow-line/edge artifacts outweighing paper stock. Passing M/L/C/Z closed-path validation is not artistic approval; inspect real film frames and browser crops at final resolution.
