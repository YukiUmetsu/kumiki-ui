## Deconstruction — macro / meso / micro

**HG — spatial / curved hemp.** Macro: three broad, partially overlapping mountains and deliberate background reserves. Meso: multiple nested curved rock backs connect to each parent ridge; long downward hemp-lift gesture families are concentrated rather than uniform vertical rain. Micro: asymmetric taper and dry root-to-tail edge. Negative control: triangle with 6 straight hairs pasted over it.

**GX — rolling cloud-head / crab-claw.** Macro: dense centrally oriented crags separated by natural mist gulfs; old trees create irregular hooked silhouettes, not pine clip art. Meso: rock ridges curl/reverse horizontally, overlap and return downward; visual dark shadows occur at geological folds. Micro: interrupted, brush-pressure-variable curl edges and short dark recession strokes. Negative control: regular row of identical semicircle scallops.

**FK — monument / raindrop.** Macro: vertically monumental weight and a white cloud break between foreground and distant massif; traveler is tiny relative to cliffs. Meso: dark overlapping cliff planes, irregular cap shelves and short dripped texture groups. Micro: individual loaded teardrop cun strokes with variable width/length and dry lift. Negative control: global speckle noise or translucent smooth mountain triangle.

**WM — recursive rock density / ox-hair.** Macro: highly packed, twisting layered rock and forest, with a consciously retained right-side release passage. Meso: narrow serpentine rock contours, many fine anisotropic dry brush fibres, vegetation and eroded stone interleave; density changes rather than filling the entire page. Micro: split dry tails, uneven small ink deposits and fine moss dots. Negative control: regular parallel comb stripes covering all mountain regions.

## Risk assessment

- **Cultural technique mixing:** do not arbitrarily combine Wang Meng ox-tail, Fan Kuan raindrops and Guo Xi cloud-heads into one named historical painting.
- **Procedural flattening:** counting marks does not prove quality. Clipped stickers and regular rows fail: inspect each native crop.
- **Scale:** A close-up with convincing lines but no legible mass at 960×540 is rejected. More high-frequency marks cannot repair missing macro anatomy.
- **Copyright:** External institutional reference images are linked for study; no museum image is embedded in the JavaScript registry, Storybook package, or video exporter.
