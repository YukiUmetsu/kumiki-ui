## Where ANAT1 enters the actual native painter

`src/video/crayon-artcraft-f1-anat1-malar.ts` fits **one orthographic 3D face camera** to the frozen source photo, with 13 independently observed valid photo-to-ANAT1 correspondences (four near/far eye corners, sellion, pronasale, menton, two mouth corners, upper/lower lip, near cheekbone and near brow). Source **face_top/hairline_center** are intentionally excluded because the ANAT1 face mask does not represent hairline anatomy. Camera: yaw −50°..−6°, pitch −12°..22°, each in 2° steps. Weighted Procrustes fits one *uniform* source-image scale, 2D roll, and translation; a roll above 18°, scale outside 1–6, or weighted camera RMSE >40 source pixels fails closed. No independently per-feature image warp is permitted.

The registered camera yields named **3D-derived source-space** points for cheekbone.R, zygion.R, gonion.R, alare.R/L and menton, and the same ANAT1 mesh yields actual surface-normal lighting from `renderMeshFrame`. The observed source cheek landmark (211,658) remains authoritative: the proposed malar crest is 65% photographed position and only 35% canonical 3D projection. A tangent from actual registered zygion/eye/cheek forms a single continuous 4-cubic asymmetric `cheek-malar-bounce` wax surface, natively parent-clipped to the original solid face. The already existing V28 pigment triplet, granular paper tooth, directional nib build, pressure, gradient, opacity, light/dark variation, timing and camera are preserved. The only paint differences are the *original named layer's* continuous cubic topology, bounds and 3D-informed directional wax angle; **zero new layers/triangles/photos/masks** are inserted.
