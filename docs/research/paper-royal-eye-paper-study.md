# Eye construction study — source observations and prototype plan

Target: a small, right-facing original paper puppet eye at final film scale, with an enlarged static study before production integration. References are for technique and anatomical observations, not to copy the artist's composition or identity.

1. Shelley Castillo Garcia, layered paper eye, [source image](https://inspgr.id/app/uploads/2019/03/art-shelley-castillo-13.jpg), SHA-256 `791fa8c99b5a3b3aea302ec34ac9d4883de0e16ec4f3449dbe6926efaa83fe16`, rights unverified; local study only. Close examination: the eye socket is a nested field of irregular cut edges, not an outlined cheek island; dark upper eyelid continues into tapered lashes that overlap the pupil; individual edges cast directional shadows. It is an extreme close-up and does not imply that every eyelash is visible in the small film shot.
2. RAW PAPER ART, [Anne in Purple](https://rawpaperart.com/portfolio), image SHA-256 `82b880e393322342aee191c8c4c9ceee4abeebff130605442c50d80a49933ee7`, rights unverified; local study only. Broad irregular bands connect the cheek, temple and jaw; neighbouring values meet as fitted cuts. The eyelids are dark tapered curves and their intersections remain distinct. This is a front view with closed eyes, so it is not a geometrical template for the puppet's profile.
3. Lotte Reiniger, [Cinderella king and queen cut-outs](https://www.bfi.org.uk/inside-the-archive/features/object-week-lotte-reiniger-cut-out-figures-backgrounds-cinderella), © Primrose Productions, licensed to BFI; image SHA-256 `db3a046e1c554fbc73772ab9b21dffc358315b2b9711f8d5a2eb43be66a65035`, local study only. The separate rigid silhouette parts overlap at visible joint fasteners; thin projections, openings in the crown and uneven lace contours remain integral to the surrounding mass. Small shot-size figures require silhouette legibility before internal detail.
4. Library of Congress [female profile photograph](https://www.loc.gov/item/99472203/), source and rights documented in paper-royal-profile-refinement.md. The inspected local eye crop is `/tmp/paper-profile-female-eye-source.png`, 650×650. It shows an upper lid occluding the dark iris, a sloped fold above the lid, narrower outer corner, and a lower lid turning into the cheek. It is a three-quarter view; the production puppet is closer to profile. Do not claim pixel or identity matching.

## Feature to construction map

| Region | Observed reference principle | Prototype geometry | Reject |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Socket and cheek | Continuous broad value changes, no closed decorative outline | adjoining large paper planes extending beyond the crop | isolated cheek sticker or contour ring |
| Eye aperture | upper/lower contours converge at distinct corners | asymmetric closed almond stock, tapered at both ends | ellipse or white triangular wedge |
| Iris and lid | dark iris tucked under upper lid | dark iris stock covered by the upper lid; narrow lower rim | full oval protruding past lid |
| Fold | lid fold arcs separately above eye | one subdued tapered arc tied to socket plane | eyebrow substituted for lid fold |
| Microdetail | raised cut edges and nonuniform tapered lashes | only a few authored tapered cuts with silhouette intersections | random parallel surface dashes |

Draw a neutral static single eye on overlapping solid stocks; review at 1× and 3× against the actual photo and paper sources. Source/actual-render landmarks, critical masks, source-matched full face and five value planes are absent; do not call this a portrait or release-gate pass. No production rigging from this study until static visual review.

## Static prototype — rejected, not product art

An original [editable eye prototype](assets/paper-royal-study/eye-prototype-unapproved.svg) and [3× browser render](assets/paper-royal-study/eye-prototype-unapproved-3x.png) test opaque paper stocks, a clipped iris and a separate upper lid. SHA-256: SVG `5c801c586b9ef0438780469bed38584be9a062a64b40c4d70c4ab327b678ba9f`; render `6c009b190cba69d804a59e23452ced2124fbe1ad13c624b8da5b386eab9f6b40`. This is isolated research, not the actual royal story or a finished component.

Independent review rejected it: the symmetrical almond and central iris do not reproduce the oblique near eye; the upper lid and brow are generic smooth ribbons, lashes are triangular, and the broad cheek bands do not follow orbital or nasal planes. A subsequent uncommitted attempt shifted the iris but detached the lash shapes from the lid; it was discarded after rendering. Reconstruct the corner and lid geometry from a matched view, preserve lash roots, and map adjacent paper planes to the actual socket and nose before another review. Source and output annotations, anatomy/structural checks and 1× full-character evidence remain open. No product geometry was changed by this study.

## Source-coordinate iteration — rejected

The next static pass uses the same `(225,55,450,225)` source crop as its 225×170 drawing coordinate space. The [comparison](assets/paper-royal-study/eye-profile-v5-comparison.png) shows the source enlarged 4× with nearest-neighbor sampling and the separately rendered candidate at 900×680. This is a qualitative view, not a registered pixel-fidelity test. The low-resolution source is soft around the inner corner and iris boundary; no exact landmark precision or unseen geometry is implied. Original source bytes and rights remain documented in [the source notes](paper-puppet-contact-source-notes.md).

The [editable v5](assets/paper-royal-study/eye-profile-v5-unapproved.svg) moved the upper lid closer to the observed sloping source, darkened the aperture and removed an unsupported highlight. The candidate's [actual browser render](assets/paper-royal-study/eye-profile-v5-unapproved.png) is SHA-256 `5df8809cb7d3d42f61cc9ef2353ca4c86da7f0d3ffdad788054c33169b2d6867`; SVG SHA-256 `b9a81a450109f64799375fc2916f912dad3321fa763aa102eb43f33c39452839`; comparison SHA-256 `8aa077173b8bb188bb118d560bd1fd3359fc3fcc10a22f28015bf61c8ad39398`.

An independent reviewer rejected both v3 and v5. V5 still has an oversized almond aperture, dominant iris, thick smooth lid, and parallel socket bands; the source's eye is oblique, the lid edge subtler and the inner region much darker. Neither candidate demonstrates the result on the full head or at native shot size. A subsequent local v6 softened the lower rim but did not resolve the construction and was discarded. The source is three-quarter, whereas the puppet uses a more side-on profile; seek a sharper, rights-cleared, view-matched source before another production attempt. No source/render landmark, semantic-mask or full-face gate has passed, and no production artwork has changed.

A closer view-direction source is Clark's [Caroline Harrison, circa 1889](https://www.si.edu/object/caroline-harrison%3Anpg_NPG.2007.293), National Portrait Gallery, Smithsonian Institution; gift of Francis A. DiMauro. The Smithsonian records the media and metadata as CC0. The 2563×3921 JPEG rendition retrieved from [the Smithsonian image service](https://ids.si.edu/ids/deliveryService?id=NPG-NPG_2007_293HarrisonM_d1&max=6000) has SHA-256 `335baec0b4991e655c82d7567900bbebf9a0b8cbd77e66e4d764cdf0d75c35f1`; the [460×510 eye crop](assets/paper-royal-study/smithsonian-profile-eye.png) at `(1440,1250,1900,1760)` has SHA-256 `2f1f612ba21cf804043703c7bb59335c3817cef06031184339aa9dd850275119`. This is an anatomical/lighting reference, not a request to reproduce the sitter's identity. It is closer to the puppet's view, but is still a different person and pose.

The actual photograph shows the eye recessed in a connected brow-to-nose shadow. Its upper lid slopes into a shallow aperture; a small partly hidden iris sits near the nose side, and the lower lid grades into the cheek. In the [v2 experimental study](assets/paper-royal-study/profile-eye-v2-comparison.png), the source is enlarged 2× with nearest-neighbor sampling, beside the independently rendered paper study. The [editable SVG](assets/paper-royal-study/profile-eye-v2-unapproved.svg) is SHA-256 `28ffc724872f249a269a6fbd2f7afeb698a8889072db1f3cc2bf603e6f1222ce`; its [render](assets/paper-royal-study/profile-eye-v2-unapproved.png) is `e86c38f8767e16a60cc47a015fa2a3eca02e282f47d6364c42c5ed30dc13c4ee`; comparison is `d65e0fcdcf8403ecad593caba1c8fb79a6bcbd1b032be55b3651d9b8ee160436`. It is a source-guided isolated crop, not a registered measurement or completed face.

An independent reviewer found that v2 connects the socket and makes the eye shallower than v1, but rejected it for production: the new shadow is an oversized smooth sheet with a pointed cut, and the upper lid and iris remain overly geometric. It does not establish full-head appearance at native shot size, measured source/render landmarks, mask correspondence, motion or artistic acceptance. Continue with a whole-head static trial and connected, nonuniform paper planes; preserve the current puppet until those checks succeed.

## Whole-head staging trial — rejected

A temporary uncommitted trial transferred the source-guided eye and a larger cheek/socket plane to the existing princess geometry. It was captured at frame 610 through the actual Storybook stage, first at [1× stage size](assets/paper-royal-study/whole-head-trial-stage-1x.png) (SHA-256 `db4d5781c0ad379ec40faee10dc5aaf3ca7818f19b0a6a669650e7e0540b6246`), then through the same stage's [3× loupe](assets/paper-royal-study/whole-head-trial-rejected.png) with device scale factor 3 (SHA-256 `fac4f5caec2d4ce4dc1c00a33ab0631a9e5358950c1aa12d54bd02a440525dd1`). The 1× capture does not provide enough pixels to assess fine anatomy.

Independent review rejected this trial. The iris is better contained, but the larger plane has a bright continuous border resembling a mask; the eye remains a white wedge under a thick arch. The renderer applies a shared cut-edge treatment to each compound layer. As a diagnostic only, a browser DOM edit removed the two strokes from the whole facial-plane layer; [this capture](assets/paper-royal-study/whole-head-trial-no-rim-diagnostic.png), SHA-256 `b532f844c92a4b824667f039b156420642a470ae8860880d890081d83f9a9ed0`, removed the mask rim but also erased the ear's edge and left the face flat. It is **not** an implementation, exact-head proof or film render.

The likely repair needs separately authored ear, socket and cheek sheets with intentional per-sheet edge treatment, followed by a source-matched still and native-size review. Do not disable the scene-wide rim to conceal the problem. The temporary production geometry was restored after inspection; the diagnostic does not pass the anatomy, structural, motion or artistic gates.

## Reusable per-sheet edge control

The diagnostic above motivated `PaperCutLayer.edgeIntensity`, a bounded per-sheet multiplier for the base cut line and edge highlight. It leaves untouched default rendering, stock opacity, apertures, fibre and shadow. The **Garden** Storybook's wing control illustrates the API; the existing artwork itself is not modified. These [zero](assets/paper-royal-study/wing-edge-zero-3x.png) and [full](assets/paper-royal-study/wing-edge-full-3x.png) crops come from Chromium device-scale-3 renders of the actual same Garden stage. Crop bounds `(770,365,1070,570)` are in each 1590×903 screenshot; hashes are `b5ea550407bd706f41498b1294bbafc044dbd0daa4ed9df50ec39c08a62e51bb` and `d04056639a026e28423da65ac5f17d7f32e9d763db0ccf35f9d215a1055a5067` respectively. A separate reviewer observed the pale rim change on the wing, with neighboring shoulder and cutouts unchanged. This verifies the control visually in these stills, not the royal artwork, facial anatomy, physical thickness or motion.

## Ear and facial-plane sheet separation

The existing princess ear contour now has its own opaque sheet parented to the head; the existing cheek, temple and nose-detail contours stay together with `edgeIntensity:0`. This preserves the ear's cut edge while suppressing the prominent rim around the facial planes. No new face contour or portrait fidelity is claimed. Tests check the ear and head share the same evaluated world matrix at frames 0, 120 and 239 in all three acts and both aspects; they do not prove every frame or motion quality.

An independent reviewer inspected actual Storybook captures at frame 610: [landscape](assets/paper-royal-study/ear-split-landscape-3x.png), SHA-256 `bf5b7d6566d598b294f6e1400f2b66d81c16e93007a79b125813fac82aef27bc`; [portrait](assets/paper-royal-study/ear-split-portrait-3x.png), SHA-256 `f9b7960043b8d3ae50d75fd14c17b619d6d272c5c41175f41ffac4c77d6e40f7`. Both use the existing 3× loupe at Chromium device scale 3. The reviewer saw the cheek's outlined sticker disappear and the ear edge remain attached in these two stills, but noted that the cheek is still a faint isolated patch, the eye remains an oversized light shape under a dark arch, and stock marks cross the face. The independent review is a narrow improvement, **not** portrait approval or a motion review. Source/render landmark and mask evidence, normal-speed motion and artistic acceptance remain open.
