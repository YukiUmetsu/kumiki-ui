### Five ArtCraft constraints carried into engineering

1. **Topology:** Every folded face is a clip of the **same complete original SVG two-drum press sheet**. No separate art per panel, no page-by-page text replacement, no raster screenshots, no source path reduction. The adjacent projected crease boundaries on either side of all 3–7 folds must match exactly in 2D coordinates at **every** absolute film frame, including the half-open and nearly-flat states.
2. **Direction/vector flow:** Alternating mountain/valley paper folds are encoded as continuous affine faces with individually sheared projection along *one selected original score axis*: vertical (left/right) or horizontal (top/bottom). A page face has fixed paper/local print coordinates; it does not change each source paint stroke's orientation separately. Continuous affine faces preserve local straight lines and ink dot geometry in sheet space.
3. **Edge and micro-element:** A scored fold has a dark impressed line, a thin pale lifted shoulder and **58 independently positioned and curved cellulose scuffs per physical fold**; microscratches stay attached to its original paper coordinate and become more visible with folding. These never become a tiled static overlay or camera-following texture. Zero paper-relief creates no visible crease effect, even though the page still folds.
4. **Depth and illumination:** Printed ink and paper respond as unified planar faces. Source vector dots, color registration, fine outlines, negative-space pore openings and original text are unchanged; a gentle *multi-stop* physical panel illumination gradient (darker at ridge/gutter than at the center) and shallow local score shadow provide readable mountain/valley depth. No blanket desaturation, single generic vignette or artificial Gaussian-blurred screen pass.
5. **Temporal continuity:** One 24fps FilmStage clip controls a closed accordion, a 144-frame unfold and a 64-frame original open editorial still. The PDF/TIFF master exists independently of this optical presentation. Deterministic source `seed` changes only physical score scuffs and fold height variations; it never regenerates the original separately screened spot inks.

## Public reusable output

- **Components:** pure `createRisoAccordionProjection`, `risoAccordionPoint`, `risoAccordionCreaseFibres`, `risoAccordionOpenness`; React `RisoAccordionPrintedZine` taking `sheet:RisoSheet`, `panels:3..8`, `axis:"vertical"|"horizontal"`, `openness:0..1`, `seed`, `paperStockRelief`, `paperGrain`, `misregistration`, optional `drumContact`, `label`, `className`, `style`.
- **Frame states:** `openness=0` is a visibly narrow concertina with 3–8 source-connected panels; `0<openness<1` gives a full SVG per-panel source clip, affine projected page and alternate lighting; `openness=1` renders precisely **one untouched complete original RisoPrint** with no crease mask or extra gradients, matching source plate layout pixel-for-pixel. `reducedMotion` finishes the original print instead of pausing on a partially folded pose.
- **New storyboard:** Original 24fps FD1 `RISO78_ACCORDION_FILM`, 3 scenes and 248 total frames, 16:9/9:16 original authored illustrations. Storybook under the existing Riso prints group with play/scrub, beat selection, 3–8 paper segments, fold axis, relief, grain, drum contact, seed, 1×/2×/3× native microscope, focal X/Y, and reduced motion.
- **Source:** Retain native print from `risoExampleSpec` and `risoExampleArtworks`, sharing the exact input across all stage faces and rendering the original in the last frame. RISO75 drum contact remains optional and independent.
- **A11y:** One descriptive outer image, hidden duplicate source ink SVGs, labeled buttons/selects/ranges, keyboard Home/End, reduced-motion still, no network, no remote assets, no new runtime deps, editable source in standalone clean `riso-print` registry package.
- **Limits:** An art-directed 2.5D overhead view; affine faces and alternating crease shading **do not compute actual 3D hinge torque, geometric collision, light transport, paper flexural rigidity, manufacturing fold tolerance or physical Risograph print fidelity.** No claims of measured print realism or press-certified full-scale sample detail.

## Acceptance and actual-picture sign-off

1. Pure geometry tests: at 0%, 10%, 25%, 42%, 67%, 99% and 100% openness every adjacent fold edge is identical as a 2D polyline and final maps exactly to original `identity` on every face. 3–8 panels and both axes; invalid dimensions, NaN, unsafe seed and frame boundaries fail closed.
2. Browser: **same original two spot masters** in four projected faces, 3 impressed scores / 174 organic scuffs; mouse-free keyboard controls, actual chromium 1×/3× closeups, no per-ink crawling on microscope-only zoom. Switch to five paper panels to expose four score lines / 232 scuffs, switch to horizontal folding, inspect 9:16 print.
3. Final output: at frame 247 and reduced motion, precisely one full-size 540×960 original print, no crease or paper shade, no source loss, original text/stamp and both ink overlays preserved.
4. Clean independent registry install `riso-print` of both new modules; strict typecheck; Storybook production build; original RISO75/76/77 tests; browser error-free; actual image capture before merger.
5. **ART HOLD** for true folding physics or calibrated real printed Risograph paper; reference photos are external. The release can be classified only as an **opt-in source-native cinematic illustration tool**, never as a validated physical print simulator.
