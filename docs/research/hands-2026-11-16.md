# Hands in detail (ANAT1) — research, 2026-11-16

The owner asked for:
- much more detail in hands;
- hand types learned from real hands and categorised: a strong man's big thick hands, a small girl's small soft ones
  and so on;
- many poses with details and actions, and animations.

Before this, the hand was part of the decimated body lattice, with seven poses. Now there is a dedicated hand:
- measured types (`hand-model.ts`);
- a skeleton with gestures, the 33 grasps of the GRASP taxonomy and keyframed actions;
- a renderer that ray-marches a smooth surface (`hand-render.ts`).

Grades:
- **reported:** quoted from the source;
- **measured:** from data in the repository;
- **estimated:** chosen within what the sources support, then checked in renders.

## How it is built

**Grasps.** `graspHand` places the object by its contact:
- on the palm for power grasps;
- between the pre-shaped pads for precision grasps;
- beside the index for side grasps;
- in the crook of the fingers for a hook.

It then opens each joint until the finger is clear of the object, and closes it until it touches, joint by joint from
the knuckle. The thumb is solved by a small search within its ranges: over the index finger's middle phalanx around a
cylinder, or onto the object's near side. The same solver places the thumb in gestures (on the index tip for OK,
across the middle phalanges in a fist).

**The surface** is a signed distance field:
- **Fingers:** rounded cones with joint bulges and palmar pads.
- **Palm:** metacarpals, the thenar and hypothenar masses, and the wrist.
- **Joins:** fingers blend into the palm (the webs) but not into one another.
- **Nails:** plates riding just proud of the skin, with a cuticle arc, a lunula and a free edge cut square, oval,
  round or almond.
- **Back of the hand:** veins and tendons on lean or old hands; dimples over a child's knuckles.

Shading:
- skin texture by region (`skin-texture.ts`): palm, fingertip pads, knuckles, back;
- the palm's flexion creases and the joint creases;
- a lighter palm;
- a shadow map traced from the key light, read softly;
- occlusion, and light scattered under the skin.

## What changed in review

| Defect | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lace-like contour bands over fingers and fists | Soft shadows marched through a field that overestimates distance | A shadow map traced from the light by the camera's robust marcher (bisection onto the surface), read with Poisson taps |
| Fingers overlapping | Base widths summed to more than the hand's breadth | Measured-plausible widths (22% of the breadth) |
| Thumb on the wrong side | The model is a left hand in its own frame | Right hands drawn mirrored, and views named by the hand's own sides |
| Nails as rings round the fingertip | The plate's width did not follow the finger's radius, and the end segment ran a radius past the fingertip | Nail width from the local radius; the end segment stops short so the rounded tip is the measured fingertip |
| Objects beside the hand, the thumb not wrapping | Fixed object positions | Contact-based placement, open-then-close fingers, the thumb solved onto the object |
| Spread brought fingers together | Inverted sign | One rule: + away from the middle finger |
| Hands of every size looking alike in a gallery | Each framed to fill its tile | `frameMm`: one scale for comparisons |

## Limits

- Fingers do not collide with one another: they blend where they meet.
- The skin does not fold or bulge under pressure.
- There is no arm: actions are wrist and finger motions.
- Animations render as draft flipbooks (about 0.6 s a frame at 200 px).
- Agent review only; no human visual review yet.
