# ArtCraft CP-F1 + FLORA1 — original rose calyx organ reconstruction

Status: experimental and opt-in (roseCalyxStudy defaults to false). **Not an approved botanical realism rendering.**

## Authentic primary references / rights

- American Rose Society, Basic Rose Terminology — https://rose.org/basic-rose-terminology/ — roses normally have five distinct protective sepals.
- University of California Riverside, Rosaceae botany lecture — https://ezcurralab.ucr.edu/sites/g/files/rcwecm3506/files/2020-05/08_rosaceae.pdf — receptacle/hypanthium, calyx, corolla and supporting stem are separate connected organs.
- American Rose Society, Rose Judging Guidelines — https://rose.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/2024-Horticulture-Judging-Guidelines.pdf — calyx and tube definitions.
- Susan Rubin original coloured-pencil climbing rose — https://www.susanrubinstudio.com/living-color-2021.html — distinct muted greens with subtle veining at petal junctions.
- Ann Swan botanical coloured-pencil painting — https://annswan.co.uk/ — layer low-pressure colour while preserving fine dry paper edges.

All references are morphology/technique observations only; no image pixels, traced vectors or photographs are redistributed. The independent original CP-F1 screenshot showed one broad green zigzag under the corolla and two oversized smooth leaves. That is an artistic anatomy rejection, not a contour source.

## Deconstruction before code

| Dimension | Native art decision |
| --- | --- |
| Five-sepal continuity | Five independently authored closed, tapered organ paths converge on the x242–252/y298–320 receptacle instead of a generic five-point star. |
| Receptacle and stem | Separately shaded volume occupies the central junction; lower tissue links to the original vertical stem. |
| Natural organ variation | Far-left, far-right, near-left, near-right and axial sepals vary in tip, orientation, overlap and curvature. |
| Local vector flow | Independent midrib, sparse lateral veins, species-aware soft yellow-green cuticle and CP-F1 paper-tooth pigment passes. |
| Temporal optics | Anatomical geometry is source-native and seek-deterministic; delayed dark, mid-value and dry pencil contacts retain separate phases. |
| Non-regression | Existing original petal, stem and foliage path, layers, seed index, optical details, timing and transforms remain byte-identical. |

## Release gates

New roseCalyxStudy flag defaults off. The unapproved old green band is replaced **only when explicitly enabled**, by six original region supports: receptacle plus five sepals. This anatomical change never affects the other original regions. Structural contract tests, both film aspect ratios, seed extremes, simultaneous lamina/lip study, native 1× and 2.15× Storybook screenshot QA are required.

Reject disconnected leaf-shaped wedges, a flat radial starburst, repeated mechanical veining, incorrect front/back occlusion, broken stem attachment, paper-break clipping or any lost source mark. Passing CI does not certify botanical realism.

**Remaining:** measured folded petal underside silhouettes, inner corolla volumetric overlaps, pinnate serrated rose leaflets/rachis/stipules, species-aware phenology, independently judged native animation and 1×/macro comparison. These are not shipped by this experimental increment.
