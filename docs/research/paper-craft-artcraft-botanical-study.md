## Applied ArtCraft structural plan

- Source-to-code method: independent palette, silhouette, topology, optical spacing and paper-shadow study. All artwork is original butterfly/fern/flower construction, not a duplicated composition or traced character.
- Nine joined fronds: a curved rachis with tapered leaflets and real perforated vein gaps. Their silhouette complexity varies by art-density control without random positions changing between frames.
- Five sculpted blooms: large/nested corollas of individually offset petals, physical shared central disk, separate bridge/center with punched pollen apertures.
- Four to seven satellite blossoms and eight connected gilded seed-sprays fill smaller negative spaces without particle-like fake detail.
- Moth: left and right independently hinged fore/hind wings, four true holes per upper wing, three per lower wing, shoulder coverts, thorax and antennas. Fore/hind materials are fully opaque.
- Material: layer-specific contact shadows, cut-card rim highlights and deterministic fibre stock. A lightbox uses transmitted-light backdrop rather than paper translucency or alpha ghosting.
- Composition uses a single uniform 720-unit construction scale, with extra-axis sprays for 16:9 and 9:16. This avoids squashing petals and wings under portrait output.
- Each piece has a stable ID; every outer/hole path is closed and stays within the PAPER1 limit of 80 independent paper layers.

## Verification and limits

Apply ArtCraft's research -> topology -> static construction -> motion -> raster QA order. Validations cover bounded geometry, holes, uniqueness, deterministic seeks, reduced-motion and dual-aspect FilmStage. Browser screenshot proof and actual exported-frame viewing are a separate gate. Source-derived five-axis masks cannot be honestly quantified when the reference is a different, copyrighted composition and no matched semantic masks were prepared. Numerical art fidelity is *not* claimed, and passing tests does not constitute professional aesthetic signoff.

The rendered motifs are SVG cinema layers, not physically manufacturable machine-cut templates with bridges, kerf calibration or V-fold linkage mechanics.

## Chromatic stock and layered relief (November 14, 2026)

The following additional sources were inspected for **techniques**, not traced. No source images or protected compositions enter the implementation.

- [Helen Musselwhite — vivid paper jungle and curiosities](https://www.creativeboom.com/inspiration/helen-musselwhite/): saturated teal/purple/pink/tangerine greens woven through deep, overlapping architectural layers. The new rainforest palette translates *color separation* into an original flower-and-butterfly scene without duplicating the artist's subjects.
- [Helen Musselwhite — vivid butterfly paper composition](https://theinspirationgrid.com/the-captivating-paper-art-of-helen-musselwhite/): wing zones made of different paper stocks, real openings and intense complementary colors. New paper-colored inner wing linings show physically through existing cut-out perforations.
- [Maud Vantours — stacked multicolor paper flowers](https://theinspirationgrid.com/layered-paper-artworks-by-maud-vantours/) and [studio description](https://www.maudvantours.com/en/about/): separate concentric color petals, spatial separation and cast shadows, rather than painted gradients. Five primary flowers now carry distinct extra outer rims, inner corollas and raised seed beads, each defined as its own stock color and depth.
- [Jen Stark — Cosmic Shift](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jen_Stark%27s_Cosmic_Shift_sculpture.jpg): chromatic contour stacking at regular physical depth intervals. Our work adds a modest independent accent ring, but neither reproduces nor simulates Stark's distinctive vortex composition.
- [Maud Vantours color/depth working method](https://www.commarts.com/features/maud-vantours): paper shapes, height and stock colors are designed before cutting. The code similarly assigns specific opaque colors and depths to individual shapes before FilmStage evaluates them.

**Construction map.** The 128-layer bounded native engine permits independent 13-petal outer flowers, inner crowns and beadwork, fern leaflet stock accents, contrasting butterfly wing aperture backings, small flower underlayers and a concentric ring. Two vivid but controlled palettes distribute warm/cool contrast across foliage, foreground flower focal points and moth wings. An adjustable `colorLayers` control disables or enables actual sheets instead of changing whole-scene saturation, and both 16:9 and 9:16 use the original uniform coordinate transform.
