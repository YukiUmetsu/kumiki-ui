# COLLAGE1 — editorial paper collage

**Registry pack:** `editorial-collage`
**Files:** `registry/video/collage/` — `collage-scene.ts` (contract + validation), `collage-geometry.ts` (noise, path flattening, paper edges), `collage-motion.ts` (keys, entrances, loops, cadence, camera), `collage-type.ts` (font roles + measured advances), `collage-puppet.ts` (paper-puppet rig), `collage-texture.ts` (board/fibre/grain PNGs), `collage-build.ts` (cached geometry + per-frame pose), `collage-kit.ts` (object builders), `collage-react.tsx` (`CollageScene`), `collage-tear.tsx` (`CollageTear`). Also installs `land-outline-110m.ts` (Natural Earth, public domain).
**Storybook:** Video production → Film Looks → Editorial Collage → **Why every clock agrees**, **Paper & print lab**, **Puppet cast lab**
**Production fixture:** `examples/film/editorial-collage.film.json` (generated by `node scripts/collage-film-export.mjs`; `--check` verifies it is current)
**Status:** implemented; artistic visual review recorded below is the agent's, **not** a human sign-off.

The look of the newsroom paper-collage explainer (the genre often called "Vox-style"): cut and torn paper on a
textured board, halftone print shading, sticker borders, masking tape, highlighter sweeps and boiling marker
annotations, condensed headlines pasted up on paper slips, paper maps with routes and pins, red string and jointed
paper-puppet characters — moved like stop-motion on twos under a smooth rostrum camera. No affiliation with or
material from any publisher; see [the reference study](../research/editorial-collage-reference-study.md).

## Contract

A scene is JSON-safe data (`CollageSceneSpec`): size, duration, seed, board material, named palette, font roles,
default cadence and boil, camera keys, drift, impact bumps, light direction, grain, and up to 600 nodes. There is no
clock, callback or remote code; `CollageScene` draws one SVG from `spec` + absolute `frame`, so any seek order gives
the same pixels. `inspectCollageScene(input)` returns every problem with a JSON path; `CollageScene` throws on an
invalid scene instead of drawing something else.

### Node kinds

| Kind | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `cutout` | Paper shape (`rect`, `ellipse`, `polygon`, `star`, `bubble`, `path`) with `edge` (`torn`, `cut`, `deckle`, `clean`), roughness, white fibre core / sticker `backing`, paper `material`, real amplitude-modulated `halftone` shading, printed `pattern` (lines, grid, dots, stripes) and stroke. |
| `text` | `headline` / `body` / `label` role type with measured word layout, wrapping, per-word or per-line paper `slip`s, word/letter/line `reveal` (drop, pop, stamp, fade, typewriter), highlighter sweep over a word range, animated `count`. |
| `marker` | Hand-drawn stroke from a path or generator (`circle`, `underline`, `arrow`, `cross`, `check`, `scribble`) as marker, highlighter, pencil or brush; draws on with `progress` and boils on twos. |
| `tape` | Translucent masking tape with torn ends. |
| `image` | User-supplied picture cut out by a shape with paper edge and backing. Combine with `print` for halftone. |
| `group` | Container for rigs and kits. Any node may set `print: { mode: "halftone" \| "duotone" \| "mono" }` to print its whole subtree through a real dot screen. |
| `map` | Equirectangular window onto Natural Earth land, torn per coastline; `water` makes it a torn paper sheet; `regions`, `graticule`; children placed by `geo: [lon, lat]` and clipped to the sheet. |
| `route` | Polyline (local or geo) with bowed legs and dashes, drawn on with `progress`. Any node can `follow` a route (or marker) path, optionally oriented. |
| `string` | Sagging red string between points or node origins. |
| `puppet` | Jointed paper figure from a `look` (skin, hair style, clothes, accessories, build, brads) and layered `actions`. Children attach to `puppet.hand-front`, `.hand-back`, `.head`, `.torso`, `.hips`. |

### Motion

Every node has rest placement, pivot, parallax `depth` (0 pins to the frame — for captions and presenters), visibility
window, `enter`/`exit` presets (`slide`, `drop`, `pop`, `flip`, `unfold`, `grow`, `fade`, `stamp`, `swing`, `draw`,
`peel`), sparse channel `keys` (x, y, rotate, scale, scaleX, scaleY, opacity, lift, progress) with per-key ease, idle
`loops` (bob, sway, spin, pulse, float, flutter, orbit), `cadence` 1–4 and `boil`. `lift` is height above the board:
the shadow moves away, widens and lightens, and the paper scales toward the lens. Shadows always fall in the scene's
light direction whatever the piece's rotation. The camera is smooth (keys + seeded drift + decaying impact bumps)
while paper steps on its cadence.

Puppets: idle breath and seeded blinks always run; `walk`/`run` (trapezoidal travel, stride length derived from the
distance so the planted foot does not skate, knee-drop hip compensation), `wave`, `point` (angle), `talk`
(quantised syllable mouth + beat gestures), `cheer`, `shrug`, `nod`, `look`, `think`, `carry`, `jump`
(anticipation/air/landing) and `turn` layer with 6-frame blends.

### Kit (`collage-kit.ts`)

`clock`, `mapPin`, `pushPin`, `stamp` (optional paper card), `speechBubble`, `clipping` (newspaper with masthead,
dateline, headline, body and highlight), `barChart`, `building` (house, tower, church, factory, station, shop),
`cloud`, `sun`, `locomotive`, `coverSheet` (a full-frame sheet that hides a cut), `blobOutline`. Builders return
plain nodes; the output can be saved and edited as JSON.

### Transition

`CollageTear` rips the outgoing frame top to bottom; the halves hinge about the foot of the tear, lift and are pulled
off with white fibre along both torn edges while the incoming scene plays underneath. Film slot `collage-tear`
(`src/video/collage-slot.tsx`) and the film builder resume the incoming shot after the tear, so no frame repeats.

### Typography

Font roles default to Anton (headline), Newsreader (body) and Geist (label) stacks with CJK fallbacks; the host loads
the faces (`src/styles/collage-fonts.css` for the demos). Word boxes come from single-glyph advances measured in
Chromium at weights 400/600/800 and interpolated, so slips and highlight sweeps fit on the first frame in SSR,
Storybook and export. Other faces fall back to the role table — give them more `padding`. Wide (CJK) glyphs measure
one em and wrap per character.

## Usage

```tsx
import { CollageScene } from "./components/video/collage/collage-react";
import { clock, mapPin } from "./components/video/collage/collage-kit";

const scene = { version: 1, id: "demo", width: 1920, height: 1080, durationFrames: 120, seed: 7,
  board: { material: "kraft" }, palette: { ink: "#1d1b19", red: "#cf3b2a" }, cadence: 2,
  nodes: [
    { id: "sheet", kind: "cutout", x: 960, y: 540, shape: { type: "rect", width: 1200, height: 700 }, fill: "#f2e9d6", edge: "torn",
      enter: { preset: "drop", frame: 0, duration: 14 } },
    { id: "title", kind: "text", parent: "sheet", x: -520, y: -60, text: "WHEN EVERY TOWN", role: "headline", size: 140, color: "ink",
      slip: { fill: "#f8f4ea", perWord: true }, reveal: { by: "word", frame: 20, stagger: 6 } },
    ...clock("clock", { x: 1500, y: 300, radius: 110, time: [{ frame: 0, hours: 11.5 }, { frame: 100, hours: 12, ease: "back" }] }),
    ...mapPin("pin", { x: 400, y: 900, dropAt: 40 }),
  ] } as const;

<CollageScene spec={scene} frame={frame} reducedMotion={prefersReducedMotion} />;
```

## Showcase and evidence

"Why every clock agrees" — six original shots, 1350 frames at 30 fps, 16:9 and 9:16: cold open (pasted-up headline,
sun, clock, cover-sheet wipe) → noon by the sun (sun follows one measured arc; each town's clock reaches noon
exactly when the sun passes over it) → the railroad problem (torn paper map, drawn route, locomotive following it,
pins and local clocks; portrait is a tracking shot) → tear → the day of two noons (cork board, fictional clipping with
highlighter, stamp, two puppets and a bubble, camera push) → one line (world map, meridian drawn, tissue hour bands
with offsets, presenter pinned to frame) → tear → payoff (clocks align, red string, headline slips, three
characters). Facts: towns kept local solar time; North American railroads adopted standard time on 18 November 1883;
the 1884 International Meridian Conference chose Greenwich. No invented statistics.

## Limits

- Paper fibre and grain are generated textures, not scanned stock; halftone print of photos is an SVG filter whose
  dot size follows luminance at a fixed screen angle.
- Puppets are side-on cutout rigs: no turnarounds, hands are discs, no lip-sync from audio (use `talk` windows timed
  to narration). Walks keep stance feet close to planted but do not solve IK.
- Maps are equirectangular and use 1:110m coastlines; no borders, no other projections.
- The film has no soundtrack yet; foley/VO cues are the next step.
- Rendering is SVG: very dense scenes (hundreds of shadowed pieces) are slower in interactive playback than in export.
