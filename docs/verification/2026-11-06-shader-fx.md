## Defects found on the way

- **Fade at the end of a scene.** An overlay faded out on the scene's last frames, so the review showed a ghost of each effect. An effect that runs to the end of its host now leaves with the host.
- **Blank image effects.** `water` and `halftone-dots` drew nothing from an SVG. WebGL returned error 1281, and Paper Shaders only logged it. The adapter now rasterizes SVG sources.
- **`water` too strong.** At the first settings it melted the whole photograph. The treatments are now much lower.
- **`evidence-reveal` was weak.** Small text sat in a large empty card. The excerpt is now the large element, and the source sits under it.
- **`environment-reveal` had no size control.** The focal subject was always 46% of the frame, and the title landed on it. `focalSize` now sets it.
- **Drift pushed text out of the safe area.** Film safe insets now allow for the growth.
- **A font race in `page-turn-story`.** The new seek gate found this, and it fails with no shader at all. The recipe waited for `document.fonts.ready`, which does not wait for a face that only a canvas uses, so a fresh page could paint the book in a fallback font. The recipe now loads its faces first.

- **Japanese text left the frame.** Each reveal step had a word margin, which added 26% to each character's width. Line breaks also cut through words, and `emphasis` could not match a term. Unspaced text now has no margin and breaks between phrases, and no line starts with closing punctuation.
- **The light theme had dark text on dark cards.** 59 fixed colours in the recipes are now mixes of the canvas and text tokens, so a scene flips as a whole. Text on an accent now follows the accent's luminance. A light film gets a darker default accent. Review warnings fell from 747 to 24 on the light test film.
- **`consequence-chain` was weak.** It had small type, a line through its cards and an empty lower half. It now uses the `CausalPath` layout, which removes about 40 lines. `data-story` and `build-and-connect` text is larger.
- **Seek order with drift, fixed.** `VideoExportStage` now drops the page's layers before each capture, so each frame rasters from scratch. A sweep of all 25 recipes with drift on then matched on 97 of 100 frames, and the other 3 were `page-turn-story`.
- **Films never used the real book.** The film capture page did not provide the WebGL book renderer, so every `page-turn-story` scene fell back to CSS strips. Their depth sorting caused the last seek differences, and strips showed mirrored text. The page now provides it, and the scene passes `[60,67,55,108,101,30]`.
- **Stale raster in seeks.** The reel failed a seek order of nearby frames. Film drift with composited children left text scaled from an old raster. Removing `will-change` and flat `translate3d` fixed it. A settled element now has no transform at all.

## Open items

- **Human review is pending.** An agent looked at the frames. That is not approval.
- **`halftone-dots` barely shows in the reel.** At `subtle`, with near-colours, it reads as a texture. It is restrained, and it may not earn its place. `standard` is much stronger.
- **Text on the waterfall.** The caption crosses the bright water. `film:review` reports it, and it stays legible to the agent's eye. A person should judge it.
- **One browser and GPU.** Output on other machines was not compared.
- **Phrase breaks in recipe body text.** Object-story captions break Japanese between phrases. Other recipes use the browser's line breaks, so a short label can still break mid-word.
