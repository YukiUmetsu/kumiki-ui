# Phase 2 verification — hybrid short, exported end to end (2026-10-22)

Exit condition from [17 — Explainer Visual Components §10](../17-explainer-visual-components.md#10-delivery-sequence--small-installable-slices): *"Hybrid sequence including real clip, 2D graphic, 3D scene and externally supplied audio; deterministic export with transparent failure reporting."*

**Result:** met for a 13-second 9:16 short, with the evidence and the limits below. Human visual and listening review is **pending**.

## What was built and exercised
- **Story:** **Video → Phase 2 Hybrid Short** (`src/video/hybrid-short.stories.tsx`). Shot timing, narration timing, audio lanes, sound effects and visual events live in one data module (`src/video/hybrid-short-data.ts`), shared by the story and the audio build.
- **Shot sequencing:** one `compileCutPlan` plan sequences three shots: footage (165 frames), a 2D route graphic (120) and a 3D phone (105). A 14-frame wipe and a 14-frame dissolve join them. The plan's renderer-neutral layers (shot, local frame, opacity, clip polygon) are drawn as HTML, so a canvas, an SVG and WebGL share one transition plan.
- **Real clip:** `src/video/assets/conveyor-1080p.webm` (1920×1080, 180 frames, VP9, 111 KB) is in-house footage generated by `node scripts/generate-conveyor-footage.mjs` from `src/video/conveyor-scene.ts`, so its geometry is known exactly.
  - It is decoded by the browser through `createVideoElementAdapter` and verified per frame. Export runs with `requireExact`.
  - **FreezeFrameFocus:** trim 45, freeze on source frame 90, push in on the label, two callouts, release, resume.
- **2D graphic:** an SVG route with 12 stops grouped by area; the truck departs on the word "loaded".
- **3D scene:** `HeroAssetViewer` with `createSmartphone`, running its `notify` action on the word "told".
- **Captions:** `alignmentToCaptionTrack` from the narration alignment, rendered by `CaptionPerformer` inside `ShortSafeFrame` (anchored at the top during the footage shot, and clear of the phone).
- **Audio:** `resolveAudioCues` checks seven links, all aligned:
  - freeze ↔ end of "scanned";
  - freeze ↔ ding (strict, zero tolerance);
  - callouts ↔ "label";
  - wipe ↔ beat 11;
  - truck ↔ "loaded";
  - dissolve ↔ beat 19;
  - notify ↔ "told".
- **Externally supplied audio:** built locally and not committed (macOS voices and generated tones).
  - **Narration:** each word synthesized with macOS `say` (voice Samantha), trimmed, and fitted into its authored slot with `atempo`. A word is sped up only if it overruns its slot, never stretched, so word timings are exact by construction.
  - **Music:** a 120 BPM bed whose beat clicks fall on the cue sheet's beat grid.
  - **Effects:** a ding and a whoosh.
  - **Mix:** built by `createAudioMixManifest` plus `audioMixFfmpegArgs`, then `npm run video:export -- --url=…&kmExport=1 --audio=mix.wav`.

## Defects found by this run and fixed
1. **FrameAccurateClip reported `ready` for a stale frame.** Right after a seek, its status still described the previous draw, until an effect ran.
   - `VideoExportStage` caught it: "Footage shows source frame 115, expected 149."
   - Status is now derived during render from a key of source frame, framing and box. It reads `pending` until that exact result is drawn, and errors count only for the request that failed.
   - This also covers framing-only changes during a push-in, where the source frame doesn't change.
2. **Captions split a clause at a length limit** ("…by area, then loaded in" / "stop order."). `alignmentToCaptionTrack` now prefers the last clause punctuation inside the group when a limit forces a break (`breakOnClause`, default true).
3. **Wrong-meaning icon:** the phone notification showed a hard-coded "$" payment icon. `createSmartphone` gained `notification.icon: { glyph, color }`; the default keeps the previous look.
4. **3D readiness during export:** `VideoExportStage` now waits for `HeroAssetViewer` to report `data-km-hero-ready` and refuses a 3D viewer that shows an error alert.
5. **Composition:** the parked truck covered the depot, the arrival chip clipped the truck, and the caption covered the phone. All three were re-laid out.

## Limits and honest notes
- **Narration quality:** the authored word slots are tight for TTS, so 24 of the 32 words had to be sped up to fit, up to about 3× on short function words. Timing is exact, but the delivery is not natural. A real voice track with real alignment output has not been exercised.
- **Ding in the final file:** the narrow-band measurement in the exported AAC is ambiguous because speech harmonics overlap 1320 Hz. Ding placement is verified at the mix stage only.
- **Synthetic footage:** the clip is generated, so its ground truth is exact. Real camera footage (H.264 or HEVC in Chrome or Safari, variable frame rate) remains untested; Playwright's Chromium cannot decode H.264.
- **Shot rendering:** CutDirector's whip and punch-in transitions move an SVG camera; this HTML-layer composition uses only wipe and dissolve.
- **Not reviewed:** human visual and listening review of `hybrid-short.mp4`. Automated checks do not certify that the result is premium.
